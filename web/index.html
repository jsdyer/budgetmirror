Read the comparison carefully

Survey amounts describe the selected reference year in that year's dollars. They are not current prices and have not been adjusted for inflation. A household's size, housing situation, location and irregular purchases can explain large differences.

These categories cover current consumption, not a complete cash-flow budget. Income taxes, pension contributions, savings, debt principal outside the survey definitions, gifts and charitable contributions are outside this comparison. Do not add a subcategory twice. Source estimates may not sum exactly because of rounding.
